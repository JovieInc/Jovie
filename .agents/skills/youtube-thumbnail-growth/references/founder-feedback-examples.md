## Generalized lessons

- A strong ingredient is not automatically a complete composition.
- A good premise does not excuse a failed portrait.
- A realistic generated prop can be explicitly locked when it strengthens verified storytelling.
- Decorative blue/red lines or accents require video-world or semantic rationale; otherwise remove them.
- Once the founder comments on a candidate, treat the reviewed version as dead unless locked. Return only the revision requested.
- Establish one Creator Appearance Profile and fixed complexion/color master before a portrait batch. Normalize skin tone and facial appearance against that reference in both per-card and contact-sheet QA; reject drift rather than accepting individually attractive but inconsistent faces.
- Relative preference is not reference approval. A founder saying one failed candidate is the best, closest, or least wrong among several does not authorize that candidate as the complexion master. When the whole comparison set is below the bar, reject every portrait/color treatment and retain only explicitly approved composition learnings.
- Evaluate every headline word against its local background at rendered mobile/feed size. Add an intentional scrim, stroke, shadow, or change the accent when contrast fails; never rely on color alone.
- A notebook is a factual prop, not permission to fabricate readable writing. Without approved exact lyrics, use no text or deliberately illegible abstract marks.
- A technically polished portrait is still a failure when the concept could advertise an unrelated video. Run semantic and visual-specificity QA before founder review.
- Do not suppress verified Featured Collaborators. A real collaborator such as Haruki may appear when material participation and a clear approved/reference-quality likeness are both evidenced; otherwise omit the face and never substitute a random one.
- Creator clothing is identity evidence too. Use approved reference/source-video wardrobe; for Tim, never invent skinny jeans unless explicitly evidenced and approved.
- Founder approval locks an asset but does not authorize upload. Upload only through Jovie after connector/UI merge, deploy, and runtime proof; verify exact channel identity/scopes and apply one approved image-to-video mapping at a time with screenshots and a ledger. Never use a direct fallback.
