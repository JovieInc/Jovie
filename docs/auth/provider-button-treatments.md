# Auth provider treatments and targets (JOV-6226)

EVENT: provider controls are branded exceptions to Jovie's 28px visual button contract. Guidance retrieved 2026-10-14; Apple remains first, with equal full-width slots and a 12px gap. Jovie-owned email CTAs retain their 28px face within reserved 44px targets.

Apple: white fill on the dark shell, black logo/title, 6px corner radius, system font at 19px and approximately 44px height (title ≈43% of height). This follows [Apple's current HIG](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/sign-in-with-apple/): white on dark backgrounds, permitted title/radius, comparable prominence, minimum 140×30px and margin ≥10% of height. The unmodified 24×44 SVG is “Logo - SIWA - Left-aligned - Black - Small.svg” from [Apple's official artwork](https://devimages-cdn.apple.com/design/resources/download/Logo-Sign-in-with-Apple.dmg), with built-in padding preserved and no added vertical padding.

Google: the light rectangular treatment uses white fill, #747775 1px stroke, #1f1f1f Google Sans Medium 14/20, 12px outer horizontal padding, 10px logo/title gap, and a fixed 20px full-color gradient G. [Current Google guidance](https://developers.google.com/identity/branding-guidelines) (updated 2026-07-07) permits custom buttons and requires these brand attributes and comparable prominence. The G's paths, masks and gradients are extracted unchanged from the light rectangular Android/Web SVG in [Google's approved asset bundle](https://developers.google.com/static/identity/images/signin-assets.zip). The 44px minimum and wrapping label accommodate accessibility. [Google Sans](https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Google+Sans) is locally hosted as a 2.2KB subset for the fixed label; its full [OFL license](../../apps/web/public/auth/OFL.txt) is included.

Targets reserve space in the shared shell/theme; providers have real ≥44px boxes and quiet actions have invisible in-flow ≥44px containers. Email CTAs reserve their existing expanded target with vertical margins. Recovery actions occupy stable grid columns during countdown/ready transitions. Native touch adjustment can retarget a gap tap despite disjoint rectangles: the shared touch-end guard compares the original coordinate's hit owner with the event owner and cancels mismatches. Keyboard activation is native, with canonical themed focus. Larger text can wrap and the shell scrolls. Inline legal prose links use the WCAG inline-text exception without expanded overlapping targets.
