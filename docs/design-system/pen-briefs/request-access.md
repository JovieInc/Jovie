Design the request-access screen for Jovie, desktop 1440 wide and mobile 390 wide, dark mode first.

A visitor arrives from the homepage while signup is invite-only. Show one clear headline saying Jovie is invite-only right now, one sentence on what they get when they're in, and a short form: email, then a role choice (artist, manager, label, other). One primary button.

When the role is artist or manager, the form grows by three short steps in the same card, one at a time, each skippable: a Spotify artist search ("Which artist are you?") that shows the matched artist's photo, name and follower count to confirm; a free-text field "What's the last thing you did to grow your fans, and how did it go?"; and a one-tap choice "What do you pay for today to promote your music?" with three options: nothing, under $50 a month, $50 or more a month. A small step counter shows where they are. The card height must not jump between steps.

After submit, the same card shows a calm confirmation with the email echoed back, the confirmed artist if they picked one, and what happens next. Include the artist search step, the spend choice step and the confirmation state as their own frames.

No stock photos, no feature grid, nothing that shifts after load. Match the type, spacing, colors and components already in this file.
