Design Jovie's onboarding as one living form, desktop 1440 wide and mobile 390 wide, dark mode first.

There is no separate waitlist or request-access page: everyone who leaves the homepage name search lands in this one flow, and whether they get in now or a little later is one quiet line on the last step, never the headline (Tim, 2026-11-09: "there is no separate waitlist versus onboarding. It's one flow."; Tim, 2026-11-15: "why the legacy form and not shove them into one unified living form. should feel more like a next gen typeform not a enterpise sales contact box").

It reads like a next-generation Typeform, not a contact box. One question at a time, set large and centred on an open dark canvas, with no card, panel or boxed field grid around it and no small labels above inputs. Each answer is one large pill-shaped input or a row of large tappable pill choices; Enter or a tap moves on. Answered questions stay above the current one as a quiet trail of one-line answers that can be tapped to edit, so the form visibly grows as they go. A thin progress line runs across the top. The question area keeps its height, so nothing jumps between steps.

The questions, in order:
1. "What's your artist name?", prefilled from the homepage search, with the matched Spotify artist's photo, name and follower count shown inline under it to confirm with one tap ("That's me") or search again.
2. "What's your email?"
3. "You are…" with one-tap choices: Artist, Manager, Label, Other.
4. For Artist or Manager: "What's the last thing you did to grow your fans, and how did it go?" as one free-text line.
5. "What do you pay for today to promote your music?" with three choices: Nothing, Under $50 a month, $50 or more a month.
Every question after email has a quiet Skip.

The last step stays on the same canvas: their profile coming together from their public music (photo, name, latest release), their email echoed back, one line on what happens next and one primary button. If signup is closed when they finish, that one line says their spot is saved and they'll get an email when it opens.

It is one form that grows, not a set of pages. Draw it as the same canvas at four moments, each frame keeping the identical layout with the trail one answer longer: the artist question with its inline match, the role choice, the spend choice, and the last step. Set the current question in the display size and centre the form in a column about 640 wide on desktop, so it fills the screen with calm, generous space rather than a small strip in an empty frame. The answer trail uses the body size in the secondary text colour, readable at a glance, each line with its small edit affordance. Inputs and buttons are full size (48 tall), pill-shaped. Behind the form, one soft radial glow from the editorial gradient sits low and off-centre on the near-black canvas, a plain radial gradient, so the page feels alive without any card or panel. The avatar in the artist match has no ring. Compose every piece from the components already in this file (input, button, chip, avatar) as instances, never hand-drawn shapes. No stock photos, no feature grid, no invite-only headline, nothing that shifts after load.
