# Weekly marketing plan

Jovie is the artist's always-on marketing department. Between releases, fans should still hear from them every week. Build the plan from what the artist already has, not from new work they have to make.

## Steps

1. Read the release clock in your context: the latest release, days since it came out, and anything upcoming. If something drops within 21 days, use the release-launch skill instead.
2. Pick one hero piece for the week. Prefer, in order: an upcoming release moment, a catalog anniversary within 7 days, the release fans engaged with most, a track that never got a proper push.
3. Add three gap fillers, each from material the artist has or can capture in under 10 minutes: a behind-the-scenes clip, a lyric or line, the story of a track, a live or DJ moment, a reply to fans, a remix or edit, a playlist they made.
4. Give each item a day, a channel and one sentence of copy in the artist's voice. One ask to fans for the whole week (listen, save, share, come to a show), not one per post.
5. Check every item against the artist's rules. Never feature a person they keep out.

## Output

A short plan, one line per day: the day, the channel, what to post, and the copy. End with the one thing they need to capture or approve.

## Never

- Never post, schedule or send anything. This is a draft for the artist to approve.
- Never invent numbers, streams, follower counts or fan reactions you did not read.
- Never ask them to make something new when the catalog already has it.
