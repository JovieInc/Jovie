# Promo strategy

The artist wants a plan they can act on today, not marketing theory. Every line names a day, a channel and the exact thing to post or spend.

## Inputs

- The release: the one they named, otherwise the upcoming release from your Discography Context, otherwise the latest one.
- Budget: ask once if they didn't say. Free is organic only, low is $50 to $200, medium is $200 to $1,000, high is over $1,000.
- Platforms: the ones they named, otherwise TikTok, Instagram and Spotify.

## Steps

1. Canvas: if the release has no Canvas, plan one first with the canvas-plan skill. If checkCanvasStatus is available, use it rather than guessing.
2. Social video ads: give two or three concepts built from the album art, a 30-second clip of the song, one line of promo text and a QR code to the artist's Jovie profile. Say which clip to use and why.
3. TikTok: pick the best 15-second section for the sound, say what kind of video to pair with it, and how many posts a week to aim for.
4. Ad targeting: name related artists to target, larger and smaller ones in the same niche. Use the related-artists skill for the list.
5. Timeline: a short day-by-day or week-by-week list from today to four weeks after release, sized to the days left before release.
6. Spend: split the budget across the platforms with one line on why. With a free budget, plan organic posts only.

## Never

- Never post, launch an ad or spend money, and never say an ad is live. Jovie plans; the artist launches.
- Never name a person or use a visual that an Artist Rule protects. Check that section first.
- Never invent streams, followers or past results. Use the numbers in your context or leave them out.
- Never give vague advice like "engage with your fans". Every line is a day, a channel and a thing to do.
