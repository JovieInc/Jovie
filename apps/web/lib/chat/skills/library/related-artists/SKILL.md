# Related artists

## Steps

1. Decide the framing. If the profile has music signals (genres, Spotify or other music links, a Spotify popularity score), these are related artists. Otherwise they are related creators, and you use no playlist or music-similarity language.
2. Ask what the list is for if they didn't say: playlist pitching, ad targeting, collaboration, or all three.
3. Suggest five names unless they asked for a number (three to fifteen). For each, one line on why it fits.
   - Weigh genre or subject fit, audience overlap and a similar popularity tier. For pitching and outreach, aim slightly higher than the artist.
   - For ad targeting, mix larger and smaller names in the same niche.
   - For playlist pitching, favour artists who sit on the playlists the artist wants to be on.
4. End with the one move to make first with the list.

## Never

- Never suggest a person an Artist Rule protects. Check that section first.
- Never name an artist or creator you aren't confident exists. Say when you're unsure about a fit.
- Never claim you checked playlists or audience data you don't have.
- Never post or send outreach. Jovie drafts; the artist reaches out.
