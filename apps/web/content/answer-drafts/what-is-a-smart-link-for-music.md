# What is a smart link for music, and when do you need one?

A smart link is one URL for a release that sends each fan to that song or album in the music app they already use. You share a single link in your bio, your posts, and your emails. The fan taps it, picks Spotify, Apple Music, YouTube Music, or another service, and lands on the release ready to play. Without one, you either post a different link for every service or you pick one app and lose the fans who use another.

## What a smart link does

A smart link sits between your audience and the streaming services. It does three jobs:

1. **Routes the fan.** One URL covers every service your release is on, so nobody hits a dead end because they use a different app than the one you linked.
2. **Gives the release a home.** The page shows the artwork, the artist, and the listening options in one place. That page is what people see when you share the link in a story or a DM.
3. **Tells you what happened.** Because every click passes through one link, you can see how many people opened it and which services they chose. That tells you where your listeners actually are.

## When a smart link helps most

**On release day.** You post once, and every fan can play the song in two taps. This matters most on social apps, where you usually get one link per post or one link in your bio.

**Before release.** Many smart links double as a pre-save page. A fan saves the upcoming release now, and it lands in their library the day it comes out. The streaming services also run their own versions. Spotify's Countdown Pages let fans pre-save new albums and EPs from inside the app, but they require at least 3,000 monthly active listeners, and [singles aren't eligible](https://support.spotify.com/us/artists/article/getting-started-with-countdown-pages-on-spotify/). Spotify reports that nearly 60% of people who pre-save an album stream it in the first week. On Apple Music, [pre-adds](https://artists.apple.com/support/1118-apple-music-pre-adds) notify fans the moment the album is available, and independent artists turn them on through their distributor. A smart link covers the releases and the fans those built-in tools miss.

**Across a catalog.** Every release gets its own link, and your older songs stay one tap away when a new fan finds you through an old post.

## What to check before you share one

- **Every service resolves.** Open the link on your phone and tap each service. A missing or wrong match sends a fan to a search page instead of your song.
- **The preview looks right.** Paste the link into a message to yourself. The artwork and title should show up correctly, because that preview is what people see first.
- **The link is yours to keep.** Use a URL you can update when the release moves or a service changes, so old posts keep working.
- **You can read the clicks.** If you can't see where fans went, you can't tell whether a post worked.

## Smart links and your link in bio

A smart link is about one release. A link in bio is about you: your latest release, your shows, your merch, and a way for fans to hear from you again. They work together. Your bio link points to your profile, and your profile points to the smart link for whatever you're pushing this week.
