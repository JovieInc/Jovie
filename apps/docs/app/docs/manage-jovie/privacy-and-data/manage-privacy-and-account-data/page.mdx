# Manage privacy and account data

<HelpOutcome>Use Jovie's shipped data controls without exposing private information or treating an irreversible deletion as routine cleanup.</HelpOutcome>

<HelpPrerequisites>

- Sign in to the account whose data you want to manage.

</HelpPrerequisites>

<HelpSteps>

<HelpStep title="Open Data & Privacy">
Go to [Settings → Data &amp; Privacy](https://jov.ie/app/settings/data-privacy). This page currently provides **Export data** and **Delete Account**.

<HelpScreenshot src="/proof/manage-privacy-and-account-data.png" alt="Jovie Data and Privacy settings with Export your data and Delete your account controls" caption="The signed-in Data & Privacy controls." />
</HelpStep>

<HelpStep title="Export a portable copy">
Choose **Export data** to download a dated JSON file. The implemented export includes account fields and settings plus owned profiles, profile links, and profile contacts. Store it somewhere private; it can contain account-private and contact information.
</HelpStep>

<HelpStep title="Correct information at its source">
Edit public artist details in **Settings → Artist Profile** and preview the signed-out profile. For account information that has no edit control, contact [privacy@jov.ie](mailto:privacy@jov.ie) and identify the field and expected correction. A request is not a promise that every value can be changed in every system.
</HelpStep>

<HelpStep title="Review what is public">
Your signed-out `jov.ie/{username}` page is the reliable test for public information. Login email, billing details, Settings, exports, and audience contacts are account-private surfaces. Do not assume every field in an export is public because it belongs to a profile.
</HelpStep>

<HelpStep title="Manage consent where the control is shown">
On a public Jovie page, **Cookie Settings** can appear under the page's consent rules. Use it to review the offered Analytics and Marketing choices; essential storage stays enabled. See the [Privacy Policy](https://jov.ie/legal/privacy) for the current policy text.
</HelpStep>

<HelpStep title="Delete only when you intend to leave">
Choose **Delete Account**, type `DELETE`, and select **Permanently Delete Account**. Jovie requires a current signed-in session, removes the account through its deletion workflow, and signs you out. The product states that this cannot be undone, so export anything you need first.
</HelpStep>

</HelpSteps>

## What happens next

An export downloads in the browser. A successful deletion signs the account out and removes access to its profile and account controls. Support may ask you to prove control of the account before discussing private data; never send a password, sign-in code, or full payment details.

<HelpTroubleshooting>

### The export does not download

Stay signed in, allow the browser download, and try once more later. Repeated requests can be rate-limited. If it still fails, report the visible error without attaching the export itself.

### Deletion does not complete

Confirm that you typed `DELETE` exactly and that the session is still current. If the request fails, the UI reports an error; stop retrying repeatedly and contact privacy support.

### Public information is still wrong

Edit the source field, save it, and check the signed-out profile again. For catalog or provider inaccuracies, use the [incorrect-data reporting guide](/docs/manage-jovie/contact-support/contact-support-or-report-incorrect-data).

</HelpTroubleshooting>
