# Account and login

- Create an account at `/signup` or sign in at `/signin`.
- Claiming a profile requires being signed in; see [find or claim your profile](/docs/jovie-essentials/find-or-claim-your-profile).
- Billing and subscription actions are under [Plans and billing](/docs/manage-jovie/plans-and-billing).
