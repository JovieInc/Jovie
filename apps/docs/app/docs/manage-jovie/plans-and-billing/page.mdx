# Plans and billing

Your subscription lives at **Settings → Billing** (`/app/settings/billing`).

- View your current plan status and available actions on the Billing page.
- `Manage in Stripe` opens the Stripe customer portal for payment-method and invoice details.
- `Compare plans` shows the current plan options; pricing shown there is authoritative.
