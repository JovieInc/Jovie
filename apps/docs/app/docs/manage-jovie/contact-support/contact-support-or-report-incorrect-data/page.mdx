# Contact support or report incorrect data

<HelpOutcome>Give support enough reproducible context to investigate while keeping credentials, payment data, and other people's private information out of the report.</HelpOutcome>

<HelpSteps>

<HelpStep title="Escalate from the closest Help Center context">
On an article, choose **No** under the helpfulness question, select the closest reason, then choose **Contact support**. From empty search results, choose **Contact support with this search**. Starting there preserves which public article or search led to the handoff.
</HelpStep>

<HelpStep title="Use the direct fallback when needed">
If the contextual link does not open, visit [jov.ie/support](https://jov.ie/support) and choose **Email Support**, or email [support@jov.ie](mailto:support@jov.ie) directly.

<HelpScreenshot src="/proof/wrong-artist-support.png" alt="Jovie support page with Documentation, Email Support, and Getting Started options" caption="The public support page is the direct fallback." />
</HelpStep>

<HelpStep title="State expected versus actual">
Begin with one sentence for each: what you expected, what Jovie showed, and the last action you took. Include the affected public Jovie URL and the page or control where the problem appears.
</HelpStep>

<HelpStep title="Add evidence for incorrect profile or catalog data">
Include the correct provider artist URL, the affected release title, and a screenshot with the wrong field visible. Add a public UPC or ISRC only when it is already visible and relevant. For analytics, include the selected date range and labels—not a list of private visitors.
</HelpStep>

<HelpStep title="Know what the handoff carries">
The contextual URL can include a public article ID and source, or the exact Help Center search phrase. It does not automatically attach your dashboard, browser logs, screenshots, account export, payment details, or a copy of the article feedback reason. Put only the necessary evidence in your email.
</HelpStep>

<HelpStep title="Remove sensitive material before sending">
Do not send passwords, sign-in codes, API keys, access tokens, full card or bank numbers, account exports, or another user's private data. Redact unrelated names, emails, phone numbers, and transaction details from screenshots.
</HelpStep>

</HelpSteps>

## What happens next

Support can use the public URLs, expected-versus-actual description, and redacted visual evidence to reproduce or route the issue. Submission does not guarantee a resolution time, a provider correction, or a specific outcome.

<HelpTroubleshooting>

### The contextual submission fails

Use the direct support page or email address above. Copy the Help Center article URL or your search phrase into the message so the lost context is restored.

### The report is too large or contains private data

Send the smallest screenshot that shows the issue. Replace private values with redaction and describe the omitted material; do not send a full account export as evidence.

### The provider and Jovie disagree

Link both public pages, name the exact field, and say which provider page you believe is authoritative. Do not claim ownership of a different artist or send that artist's private information.

</HelpTroubleshooting>
