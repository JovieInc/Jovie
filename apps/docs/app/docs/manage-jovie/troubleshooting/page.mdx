# Troubleshooting

## How do I get started with Jovie?

Start with **Find yourself** and follow the setup steps for your profile.

## How do music smart links work?

When you add a release, Jovie generates a smart link for it. The link routes a
fan to a specific streaming platform only when the URL carries an explicit
`dsp` parameter or the fan has a stored `jovie_dsp` preference cookie;
otherwise the release page renders without an automatic redirect.

## How do I upgrade my plan?

Head to **Settings → Billing** to view available plans and manage your
subscription.

## How do I contact support?

Use [Contact support](/contact) to email the team with the page and search
context attached, or email
[support@jov.ie](mailto:support@jov.ie) directly.
