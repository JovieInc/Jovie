# Fix the wrong artist or catalog

<HelpOutcome>Stop a bad artist match before it is added, or send a reproducible catalog mismatch to support for review.</HelpOutcome>

<HelpSteps>

<HelpStep title="Confirm what is mismatched">
Compare the Jovie profile with the provider page. Treat another artist's image, biography, provider URL, or releases as a mismatch even when the artist names are identical.
</HelpStep>

<HelpStep title="Reject an unconfirmed suggestion">
In **Identity → Suggested**, choose **Not me** beside an incorrect identity. This is the safe self-service correction before a suggestion is attached.

<HelpScreenshot src="/proof/wrong-artist-not-me.png" alt="Suggested Connections row with the Not me action for an incorrect artist identity" caption="Reject an incorrect suggestion instead of adding it." />
</HelpStep>

<HelpStep title="Separate editable profile details from provider data">
For your claimed profile, you may edit your own display name, username, image, location, hometown, and career highlights under **Settings → Profile**. Do not rewrite those fields to disguise a wrong provider identity, release, or catalog owner; those require review.
</HelpStep>

<HelpStep title="Send the mismatch to support">
Open [Support](https://jov.ie/support), choose **Contact Support**, then **Email support with this context**, and include the Jovie profile URL, incorrect provider URL, expected artist URL, affected release title, and a screenshot. Include UPC or ISRC only when it is visible to you.

<HelpScreenshot src="/proof/wrong-artist-support.png" alt="Jovie Support page showing the Contact Support card and action" caption="Send both the incorrect and expected identities so support can reproduce the mismatch." />
</HelpStep>

</HelpSteps>

## What happens next

Support reviews the supplied identity and catalog evidence. Keep the incorrect URLs available until the review is complete; no fixed resolution time is promised.

<HelpTroubleshooting>

### The wrong suggestion was already added

Do not add a second identity as a workaround. Send the attached provider URL and expected provider URL through **Contact Support**.

### Another person has claimed the profile

Do not edit or attempt repeated claims. Include the claimed Jovie URL, your correct Spotify artist URL, and proof context in the support email.

</HelpTroubleshooting>
