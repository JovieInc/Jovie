# Integrations

Where external services connect today:

- **Music providers** (Spotify, Apple Music, YouTube) connect from Library → Releases; see [connect your music accounts](/docs/jovie-essentials/connect-music-accounts).
- **Settings → Connections** (`/app/settings/connectors`) is for Gmail and Google Calendar, not music providers.
- Older guides for ad pixels, retargeting, Bandsintown, and tour-date sync were retired because their instructions no longer match the product; they will return here once their steps are verified against the current app.
