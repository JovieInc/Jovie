# Connect your music accounts

<HelpOutcome>Confirm the correct Spotify, Apple Music, or YouTube Music public identity, then import from the providers Jovie currently supports in Work.</HelpOutcome>

<HelpPrerequisites>

- A claimed artist profile. Music identity review is in **Identity**; imports are in **Work**. **Settings → Connections** authorizes Google services, not music providers.

</HelpPrerequisites>

<HelpSteps>

<HelpStep title="Open Identity">
From your artist navigation, open [Identity](https://jov.ie/app/presence), then choose **Suggested**. Jovie groups detected public identities for review.

<HelpScreenshot src="/proof/connect-music-accounts.png" alt="Identity workspace on the Suggested tab with Add and Not me actions for detected music identities" caption="Review detected identities before adding them." />
</HelpStep>

<HelpStep title="Confirm the exact artist identity">
Compare the provider, artist name, destination URL, and catalog. Choose **Add** only for your artist; choose **Not me** for a same-name or unrelated result.
</HelpStep>

<HelpStep title="Connect the matching Spotify artist">
Spotify is connected during profile claim or initial setup. At **Pick the exact Spotify artist**, choose the identity that owns the claimed profile; if your claimed profile has no Spotify identity, contact support instead of choosing a nearby result.

<HelpScreenshot src="/proof/connect-spotify-artist.png" alt="Pick the exact Spotify artist showing candidate names, follower counts, and Use actions" caption="The selected Spotify identity controls which catalog is imported." />
</HelpStep>

<HelpStep title="Start a supported import">
Open [Work](https://jov.ie/app/library) and choose **Add** in the toolbar. Use **Sync from Spotify** when it is available for the connected profile, or **Import YouTube** (shown as **Connect YouTube** until the channel is authorized) to authorize and import from your YouTube channel. Spotify, Apple Music, and YouTube Music can appear as detected public identities; Apple Music has no standalone authorization control in the current workspace.
</HelpStep>

<HelpStep title="Let processing finish">
Keep the selected identity unchanged while Jovie shows its import or processing state. Review Work when processing completes; Jovie does not promise a fixed completion time.
</HelpStep>

</HelpSteps>

## What happens next

Accepted identities appear in Identity, and supported imported items appear in Work after processing.

<HelpTroubleshooting>

### The suggested artist is wrong

Choose **Not me**. If a wrong identity is already attached, follow [Fix the wrong artist or catalog](/docs/manage-jovie/troubleshooting/fix-wrong-artist-or-catalog); do not add another same-name result to compensate.

### YouTube asks for permission again

Choose **Import YouTube** and complete authorization with the channel owner account. If Jovie shows **Reconnect YouTube to refresh access**, reconnect before retrying the import.

### An import stays in progress or fails

Do not switch to another artist identity. Retry the visible provider action once; if it still fails, contact support with the profile URL, provider, and screenshot. No fixed processing-time promise applies.

</HelpTroubleshooting>
