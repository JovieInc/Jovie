# Share your Jovie link

Your public profile lives at `jov.ie/yourname`.

- Open the profile action menu in your dashboard and use `Copy Link`; `Open Profile` previews the live page.
- Paste the URL anywhere: socials, bios, emails, or messages.
- To share a specific release instead, use the release-level `Copy Link` covered under [Releases and smart links](/docs/build-your-presence/releases-and-smart-links).
