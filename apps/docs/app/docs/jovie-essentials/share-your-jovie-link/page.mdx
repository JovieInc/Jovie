# Share your Jovie link

<HelpOutcome>
Copy your public profile URL — `jov.ie/yourname` — and share it with fans.
</HelpOutcome>

<HelpPrerequisites>

- A published profile. If yours is not live yet, [publish your profile](/docs/jovie-essentials/publish-your-profile) first.

</HelpPrerequisites>

<HelpSteps>

<HelpStep title="Open the profile menu">
In your dashboard sidebar, hover over or open your profile entry to reveal
the action menu.
</HelpStep>

<HelpStep title="Copy your link">
Choose **Copy Link**. Jovie copies your full public URL
(`https://jov.ie/yourname`) and confirms with a **Copied** notification. If
copying fails, a **Failed to copy** notification appears instead — try again
or use the address bar method below.

<HelpScreenshot
  src="/proof/share-your-jovie-link-copy.png"
  alt="The dashboard profile action menu with Copy Link selected and the Copied confirmation"
  caption="Copy Link in the profile action menu copies your public URL."
/>
</HelpStep>

<HelpStep title="Check what recipients see">
Choose **Open Profile** in the same menu to open your live page in a new tab,
exactly as fans see it. On mobile the same menu is available from your
profile entry in the sidebar.
</HelpStep>

<HelpStep title="Share it">
Paste the URL anywhere: social bios, messages, emails, or a link-in-bio slot.
</HelpStep>

</HelpSteps>

## What happens next

Anyone with the link sees your public profile. To share one release instead,
use the release-level link covered in
[Add a release or create its link](/docs/build-your-presence/releases-and-smart-links/add-release-or-smart-link).

<HelpTroubleshooting>

### The copied link opens the wrong or an old page

Your URL follows your username. If you recently changed it, copy the link
again — the menu always copies the current URL. Profiles you no longer own at
old handles are deactivated automatically.

### Copy Link reports a failure

Browser clipboard permission can block the copy. Allow clipboard access and
retry, or open the profile with **Open Profile** and copy the address bar.

</HelpTroubleshooting>
