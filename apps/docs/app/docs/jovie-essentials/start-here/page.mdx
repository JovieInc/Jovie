# Start here

New to Jovie? This is the whole launch path in order:

1. [Find or claim your profile](/docs/jovie-essentials/find-or-claim-your-profile) — search for your artist name at [jov.ie/start](https://jov.ie/start). If a profile already exists for you, claim it; otherwise create one.
2. [Connect Spotify, Apple Music, or YouTube](/docs/jovie-essentials/connect-music-accounts) — import your releases from Library.
3. [Complete your profile](/docs/build-your-presence/profile-and-identity/edit-profile) — set your name, bio, images, and links in Artist Profile settings.
4. [Publish your profile](/docs/jovie-essentials/publish-your-profile) — publication follows profile completion; there is no separate Public toggle.
5. [Share your Jovie link](/docs/jovie-essentials/share-your-jovie-link) — copy `jov.ie/yourname` from the profile menu and post it.

Each step links to its own article when you need the details.
