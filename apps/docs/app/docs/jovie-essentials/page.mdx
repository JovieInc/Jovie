# Jovie essentials

The launch path for every artist on Jovie:

1. [Start here](/docs/jovie-essentials/start-here) — the short version of the whole setup.
2. [Find or claim your profile](/docs/jovie-essentials/find-or-claim-your-profile) — locate an existing profile or start a new one.
3. [Connect Spotify, Apple Music, or YouTube](/docs/jovie-essentials/connect-music-accounts) — bring your catalog onto your page.
4. [Complete your profile](/docs/build-your-presence/profile-and-identity/edit-profile) — name, bio, images, and links.
5. [Publish your profile](/docs/jovie-essentials/publish-your-profile) — make the page public.
6. [Share your Jovie link](/docs/jovie-essentials/share-your-jovie-link) — put `jov.ie/you` everywhere.
