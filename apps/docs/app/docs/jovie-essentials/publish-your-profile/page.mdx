# Publish your profile

<HelpOutcome>
Make your Jovie profile publicly available at `jov.ie/yourname`.
</HelpOutcome>

<HelpPrerequisites>

- A Jovie account.
- A profile with a username and a display name.

</HelpPrerequisites>

<HelpSteps>

<HelpStep title="Claim or create your profile">
Start at [jov.ie/start](https://jov.ie/start) and use **Find yourself** to
claim an existing profile with **Claim Profile**, or sign up and create a new
one. Claiming a profile marks it public — there is no separate `Public`
toggle to find.
</HelpStep>

<HelpStep title="Finish onboarding">
Complete the onboarding steps through to the end. A profile becomes
publishable once it has a username, a display name, and completed onboarding.
</HelpStep>

<HelpStep title="Verify the live page">
Open `jov.ie/yourname` in a private or signed-out browser window. If your
profile loads, it is published. You can also use **View as Visitor** from
**Settings → Artist Profile** to open the same page.

/>
</HelpStep>

</HelpSteps>

## What happens next

Your profile stays live at `jov.ie/yourname` and updates as you edit it.
Next, [share your Jovie link](/docs/jovie-essentials/share-your-jovie-link)
or [add a release](/docs/build-your-presence/releases-and-smart-links/add-release-or-smart-link)
so fans have something to listen to.

<HelpTroubleshooting>

### Your profile URL shows a not-found page

The profile is not publishable yet. Return to `/start` or your dashboard and
finish any remaining onboarding steps, then confirm your profile has both a
username and a display name under **Settings → Artist Profile**.

### You claimed a profile but cannot edit it

Make sure you are signed in with the same account you used to claim it. If
the problem continues, contact support.

</HelpTroubleshooting>
