# Find or claim your profile

Jovie may already have a profile for your artist name.

- **Find your profile** — search your artist name at [jov.ie/start](https://jov.ie/start) and open the matching public profile.
- **Claim your profile** — if a profile exists but is not yours yet, use the claim action on it and complete verification to take ownership.
- **Start fresh** — if nothing matches, create a new profile from the same start flow.

Claiming is tied to your account login, so have your sign-in ready before you start.
