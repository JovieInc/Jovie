# Find your Jovie profile

<HelpOutcome>Find the public Jovie profile tied to your exact Spotify artist identity without claiming the wrong artist.</HelpOutcome>

<HelpSteps>

<HelpStep title="Start with your artist name">
On the Jovie landing page, enter the name fans see on Spotify in **Search your name**, then choose **Find me**. You can also open [Start](https://jov.ie/start) directly.

<HelpScreenshot src="/proof/find-your-profile-search.png" alt="Jovie artist search with the Search your name field and Find me button" caption="Start with the public artist search." />
</HelpStep>

<HelpStep title="Compare the Spotify results">
At **Pick the exact Spotify artist**, compare the artist name, image, follower count, and popularity. Use **Search Spotify artists** to make the query more specific.

<HelpScreenshot src="/proof/find-your-profile-results.png" alt="Spotify artist results showing names and follower counts under Pick the exact Spotify artist" caption="Choose only the Spotify identity you can verify." />
</HelpStep>

<HelpStep title="Select only a trustworthy match">
Choose the row that points to your real Spotify artist page. If none is trustworthy, choose **None of These** and search again; do not select the nearest name.
</HelpStep>

<HelpStep title="Open the public profile without claiming it">
Viewing a result or a public `jov.ie/{username}` page does not transfer ownership. A separate **Claim Profile**, **Continue Claim**, or **Verify & Claim** action starts a claim.
</HelpStep>

</HelpSteps>

## What happens next

You can view and share the public profile. To edit an unclaimed profile, complete [Claim your profile](/docs/jovie-essentials/find-or-claim-your-profile/claim-your-profile).

<HelpTroubleshooting>

### No exact result appears

Search the artist name exactly as Spotify displays it, or paste the Spotify artist URL into the Start chat. If Jovie still cannot identify a trustworthy match, stop and [contact support](/docs/manage-jovie/contact-support).

### Several artists have the same name

Open the Spotify pages and compare artwork, releases, genres, and follower counts before selecting one. A shared name alone is not proof.

</HelpTroubleshooting>
