# Edit your name, bio, images, and links

<HelpOutcome>
Update the identity details fans see on your public Jovie profile from the
Artist Profile section of Settings.
</HelpOutcome>

<HelpPrerequisites>

- A Jovie account with a claimed profile.

</HelpPrerequisites>

<HelpSteps>

<HelpStep title="Open Artist Profile">
From your dashboard, open **Settings** and choose **Artist Profile**
(`/app/settings/artist-profile`). The page opens on the **Artist** section
with the **Profile** panel.
</HelpStep>

<HelpStep title="Update your photo and identity fields">
Select **Profile picture** to upload a new image — JPEG, PNG, WebP, AVIF,
HEIC, or TIFF up to 25 MB. Jovie profiles look best at 512×512 or higher; a
low-resolution photo shows a warning with its current dimensions. Edit
**Display name**, **Username**, **Current location**, **Hometown**, and
**Career highlights** (up to 2,000 characters) in place. Your username sets
your profile URL, shown next to the field.
</HelpStep>

<HelpStep title="Let each field save">
Fields save automatically when you leave them. The panel header shows
**Saving…** while a change is in flight and **Saved** when it completes. If a
field cannot be saved, an error notification explains why.

<HelpScreenshot
  src="/proof/edit-profile-artist-settings.png"
  alt="The Artist section in Settings showing the Profile panel with Display name, Username, location fields, and a Saved status"
  caption="The Profile panel in Settings → Artist Profile after edits save."
/>
</HelpStep>

<HelpStep title="Manage links and extras">
Use **Links, music &amp; more** to manage the social links, music, tips, and
about info shown on your public page.
</HelpStep>

<HelpStep title="Preview the public page">
Select **View as Visitor** at the top of the **Artist** section to open your
live profile at `jov.ie/yourname` in a new tab.
</HelpStep>

</HelpSteps>

## What happens next

Saved changes appear on your public profile at `jov.ie/yourname`. Some
details — like your photo and display name — may have been prefilled from a
connected music account; your edits in **Profile** take precedence. When your
profile looks right, [share your Jovie link](/docs/jovie-essentials/share-your-jovie-link).

<HelpTroubleshooting>

### A field will not save

**Display name** and **Username** are required — empty values are not saved.
**Hometown** must differ from **Current location**; fix the values and leave
the field again to retry the save.

### The photo upload fails

Check that the file is a supported image type (JPEG, PNG, WebP, AVIF, HEIC,
or TIFF) and under 25 MB, then try again. If the failure repeats, contact
support.

### Your changes do not show on the public page

Reload `jov.ie/yourname`. The public page is cached briefly, so a fresh save
can take a moment to appear.

</HelpTroubleshooting>
